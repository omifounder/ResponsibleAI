# SafeAI Guardian — Master Presentation Script

**Version:** 1.0 — Editable working draft  
**Target duration:** Approximately 2 minutes 45 seconds  
**Format:** Voice-over narration + visual demonstration  
**Note:** Reconstructed from previous discussions; not a verbatim transcript.

| Time | Voice-over narration | What the judges see / what we demonstrate |
|---|---|---|
| **0:00–0:20** | AI agents are moving beyond answering questions. They make decisions, access information, use tools and collaborate with other agents. But as they become more autonomous, an important question emerges: Who is watching them? | Establish the futuristic open-air retail district. People and agents interact. White activity text shows ordinary actions. **Demonstrates:** Multi-agent ecosystem. |
| **0:20–0:40** | Meet SafeAI Guardian — an independent supervisory layer for autonomous AI ecosystems. It continuously monitors behaviour, evaluates interactions and identifies emerging risks before they turn into harmful actions. | Reveal Guardian View and cyan floor sensors. **Demonstrates:** Continuous supervision. |

Beta examines interactions — who is asking, who is responding and who could be affected. | **2:35–2:45** | SafeAI Guardian. Autonomous agents need oversight. Their overseers need it too. | Return to wide district and product identity. **Demonstrates:** Product proposition. |

## Presentation principles

- Use the approved cinematic smart-retail environment as the visual reference.
- Bottom UI is one black chronological conversation/activity panel with white/yellow/orange/red text.
- No top summary cards, scoreboards, timestamps or repeated agent numbers in the panel.
- Guardians are supervisory processes, not humanoid characters.
- Demonstrate a real action gate, not just a warning.
- Gamma verifies actual enforcement.
- Accurately label simulated or mock components.

## Closing

**Who watches the agents? The Guardians. Who watches the Guardians? Each other.**

**Autonomy needs oversight. Oversight needs accountability.**
