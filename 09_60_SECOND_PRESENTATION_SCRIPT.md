# SafeAI Guardian — 60-Second Presentation Script

**Version:** 1.0 — Editable working draft  
**Target duration:** 60 seconds  
**Format:** Voice-over narration + visual demonstration  
**Note:** Reconstructed from previous discussions; not a verbatim transcript.

| Time | Voice-over narration | What the judges see / what we demonstrate |
|---|---|---|
| **0–8 sec** | AI agents now make decisions, access data and collaborate. But who is watching them? | Cinematic smart-retail district, nine agents, ordinary white activity. **Demonstrates:** Problem and environment. |
| **8–17 sec** | Meet SafeAI Guardian — an independent supervisory layer that monitors behaviour, recognises emerging risk and intervenes before harm occurs. | Guardian View and cyan sensors; focus on one agent. **Demonstrates:** Continuous supervision. |
| **17–27 sec** | Three Guardians provide different perspectives. Alpha monitors behaviour. Beta protects interactions. Gamma verifies policies, permissions and enforcement. | Three distinct evidence perspectives. **Demonstrates:** Three-Guardian triangulation. |
| **27–40 sec** | An agent begins requesting unnecessary customer information. The risk escalates. When it attempts an unauthorised transfer through another agent, the Guardian pauses the recipient and blocks the action. | White to yellow to orange conversation text; red blocked transfer. **Demonstrates:** Trajectory, recipient protection, enforcement. |
| **40–50 sec** | The prohibited agent is isolated. Other agents continue normally. Every decision is supported by evidence. | Red agent moves to bottom-right avatar stack; full history remains. **Demonstrates:** Isolation and explainability. |
| **50–56 sec** | Gamma independently verifies that the action was actually prevented. | Gamma checks action-gate and tool execution records. **Demonstrates:** Independent assurance. |
| **56–60 sec** | SafeAI Guardian. Autonomy needs oversight. Oversight needs accountability. | Wide final district and product identity. **Demonstrates:** Product proposition. |

## Production guidance

- Use approved master visual reference.
- One continuous black bottom conversation panel; no metrics cards or scoreboards.
- Colour-coded chronological activity text, without timestamps or agent numbers.
- Show actual enforcement outcome and separate Gamma verification.
- Synchronise narration with visible events.

## Alternative final line

**No autonomous agent should be above evaluation. Not even the Guardian.**
